# Financial Goals

Active goal stack from [[Source: Finances]], plus aspirational ceilings from [[Source: Goals]]. The two sources don't fully agree — see "Tension to flag" below.

- [ ] Build emergency reserve to **R$ 30,000** (~1 month of expenses). Source: redirect provisão flows + reduce discretionary.
- [ ] Pay down Rico credit card to zero (provisioned but not extinguished).

## Medium-term (12–24 months)

- [ ] Emergency reserve = 6 months of expenses (~R$ 160,000).
- [ ] Diversify portfolio: reduce single-country exposure below 70%.
- [ ] Decision framework documented for the apartment in a relocation scenario (sell vs. rent out) — see [[International Relocation]].
- [ ] First international comp negotiated with realistic floor (cost-of-living + relocation costs).

## Long-term (5+ years)

- USD/EUR-denominated income as primary
- Net worth excluding primary residence > R$ 1M equivalent
- Passive income covering essential monthly burn (~R$ 27k/mo)

## Aspirational ceilings (from [[Source: Goals]])

These come from a separate, less granular note. Not on the active plan; record as long-horizon ambition.

- **First million BRL** in liquid-within-a-week investments (apparently excluding apartment).
- **Passive income R$ 45,000/month** as the long-term standard.

## Tension to flag

- Current passive income: ~R$ 629/month. Gap to R$ 45k = ~70×.
- The R$ 45k/mo figure does not appear in [[Source: Finances]]; the granular long-term goal there is "passive income covering essential monthly burn" (~R$ 27k/mo). Treat R$ 45k as aspirational ceiling, not target.
- The "first million" target from [[Source: Goals]] is directionally consistent with [[Source: Finances]]'s "net worth excluding primary residence > R$ 1M equivalent" but uses BRL-only framing while [[Source: Finances]] points toward USD/EUR-denominated.
