# Source: Yandeh PJ Proposal

Offer letter sent by Yandeh to Yago, model PJ. 11-slide branded PDF.

## Summary

Yandeh formal offer: **PJ R$ 42.000/month base** + **bonus up to 6x salaries** tied to goal attainment (conditional, proportional to start date and to permanence on payment date). Work model presencial (Itaim, SP), currently hybrid 4 days/week per area agreement. Benefits package includes Flash R$ 1.100 (meal/food), Wellhub, SulAmérica Saúde Executivo R1 Apto (no monthly fee, coparticipation only), Allya discounts, Day Off in birthday month, parental leave (gestation 180d / paternity 30d), and mobility allowance R$ 360 (hybrid + RMSP). The letter stated start date 01/06/2026; the actual agreed start is 15/06/2026, renegotiated after issuance.

## Key takeaways

- **Comp**: R$ 42.000/month PJ model. No equity component.
- **Bonus**: up to 6x salaries, conditional (goals + permanence at payment) — upside, not base.
- **Address**: Av. Santo Amaro, 48 — Itaim, São Paulo.
- **Work model**: in-person; current practice hybrid 4 days/week as agreed with the area.
- **Health**: SulAmérica Saúde Executivo R1 Apto — titular + dependents; no monthly fee; coparticipation only for consults and simple exams.
- **Meal/food**: Flash R$ 1.100/month, credit-at-sight model.
- **Wellness**: Wellhub (8 plans available).
- **Discounts**: Allya (employee discount network).
- **Time off**: Day Off in birthday month; **24 days annual vacation** (confirmed verbally by Yago, not in the letter).
- **Parental leave**: gestation 180d, adoption 180/120/30d (by child age), paternity 30d.
- **Mobility allowance**: R$ 360/month (hybrid + RMSP resident).
