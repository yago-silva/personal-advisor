# Source: Investment Thesis

1. **Dividend aligned with growth** — slow, stable, public-company dividend payers. Inspired by Luiz Barsi / AGF methodology. Holdings: BBAS3, BBSE3, ISAE4, CXSE3.

## Notable observations (advisor flags, not in source)

- **B2C churn 9-11%/month is high**. At 10% monthly compounded, annual retention ≈ 28% — most customers gone within 12 months. LTV of 8-12 months is consistent with this. This is the structural reason FP wants B2B to dominate (B2B churn typically 1-3% / month).
- **Revenue per employee ≈ R$ 125k/year** — low for SaaS (US benchmark $200k+), suggests high-touch services component.
- **B2B is 6 months old (Nov 2025 launch)** with 8% of revenue. Going to 30% in 2026 means B2B revenue must roughly **4x** in 12 months while keeping the rest stable. Aggressive but not impossible.
- "The company does not have a clear vision about other markers, for example: churn rate" — Yago's own language. Suggests metric instrumentation gap inside FP. Worth probing if it limits valuation in any future M&A diligence.
- **13% YoY MRR growth is materially below the rate that produces 5-10x exits** (added 2026-05-04 13:31). SaaS companies that hit 5x+ outcomes typically grow 40-80% YoY. At 13% compounded:
  - 3 years: 1.44x revenue (R$ 15M → R$ 21.6M ARR)
  - 5 years: 1.84x revenue (R$ 15M → R$ 27.6M ARR)
  - For Yago's stake to 5x at exit purely on growth: would require revenue to 5x → ~14% per quarter / ~70% per year. Not happening at 13% YoY.
  - The remaining path to 5-10x relies almost entirely on **multiple expansion** (e.g. AI premium) rather than fundamentals — much riskier framing.
- **+26% sales per seller** is a positive efficiency signal — it means the team is getting more productive without proportional headcount growth. But it cannot, by itself, drive a multiple-expansion exit thesis without ARR scale catching up.
- **The B2B push at 30% target makes the math less hopeless.** If B2B grows 4x while B2C holds, blended growth could be in the 30-40% YoY range — closer to the multiple-expansion zone. But that's the *plan*, not the *track record*. Watch trajectory through 2026.
