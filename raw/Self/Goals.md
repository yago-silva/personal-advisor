## Finances

### First million in reals (BRL) — primary goal

**This is the main financial goal.** Everything else is subordinate to it: reaching a net worth of one million reals (R$ 1.000.000,00).

**Definition (unchanged):** only what can be made liquid within a week counts — stocks (Brazil or abroad), money in bank accounts, emergency reserve, other liquid investments. **The apartment and the vehicles do not count**, so the headline "patrimônio líquido" figure in the net worth sheet overstates progress toward this goal by a wide margin. Track it against `Investimentos não comprometidos` in [Patrimônio Líquido](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13cod5YUveCILRhjTISBC6xe8za1xNRLSjFkRw4edFxg/) → `Resumo`.

Set as the explicit priority on **2026-09-05**, alongside pausing the relocation plan ([[raw/Plans/Relocation|Relocation]]). Two consequences that follow directly:

- Capital that leaves the liquid bucket sets the goal back — depreciating assets and dollar-hedging for a paused move both count as leaving it.
- The lever is the gap between income and burn. See [[raw/Finances/Cash Burn|Cash Burn]] for how burn is measured and why the derived targets move with it.

### Passive Income — long-term, not the current target

Building passive income that sustains a high quality of life while the capital keeps growing. The aspirational threshold is **R$ 45.000,00/month**; the floor that actually matters is **full replanned monthly burn covered by proventos** — discretionary lifestyle included, not essential-only.

This remains a real goal, but it is **downstream of the first million**: the capital required to throw off that income is several multiples of the million, and it moves further away every time burn rises. Not the thing being optimized right now.
