## Dividend aligned with growth

The idea behind these investments is to focus on generating consistent monthly income. This approach is based on [Luis Barsi's ](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luiz_Barsi_Filho) investment philosophy and the [AGF](https://acoesgarantem.com.br/) methodology.  
The goal is to receive dividends from stable, reliable stocks (from public companies) that offer some growth potential—typically slower than small-cap stocks.

In my wallet the investiments that follow these thesis are: BBAS3, BBSE3, ISAE4 and CXSE3

### Fluencypass

As Fluencypass is a private company, financial information about it is not publicly available online.

Fluencypass is based in recurrency (from B2B and B2C)

**COMPANY SIZE**: ~120 employess
**MRR:** ~ R$1,250,000
**LVT**: ~ 8 - 12 months
**CHURN  B2C**: 9% - 11% Month
~ 10.000 active B2B Subscriptions
~ 55 B2B Contracts

Approximately 82% of this revenue comes from B2C subscriptions, 10% from the study abroad product, and 8% from B2B contracts.

B2B is Fluencypass’s main focus for 2026. Sales of this product began in November 2025, and it currently represents about 8% of total revenue. The goal for 2026 is to increase B2B to 30% of total revenue while maintaining the current revenue levels of the other segments, meaning growth will be driven primarily by the expansion of the B2B product.

The company does not have a clear vision about other markers, for example: churn rate
